# GhostScore: 3-minute demo script

Target length: 3:00. The narration is about 260 words, roughly 2 minutes of speech at 130 words per minute. The other minute is for clicking, page loads and short pauses. Time a dry run, since it is a rough estimate.
Persona: Priya (synthetic). Everything runs in local mode with labelled stubs.

## Before you record

1. Start the app: `cd ghostscore && GHOSTSCORE_MODE=local .venv/bin/uvicorn main:app --app-dir backend --port 8000`
2. Restart it right before recording so history and audit are clean (state is in memory).
3. Open in a phone-width browser window (about 420 px wide):
   `http://localhost:8000/ui/stitch/stitch_ghostscore_credit_advisor_app/ghostscore_welcome_consent/code.html`
4. Open a new private window or clear site data, so earlier uploads do not appear.
5. Have the deck open on slides 6 (architecture) and 9 (limits) in another window.
6. Do one dry run. Use the **Use sample** buttons, not real files (extraction is a stub).

## Script

| Time | On screen | Say |
|------|-----------|-----|
| 0:00-0:20 | Welcome screen | "Meet Priya. She pays her rent, her electricity and her UPI bills on time, every month. But she has never taken a loan, so a credit bureau sees nothing. GhostScore turns the payments she already makes into a credit-readiness score she can understand and improve." |
| 0:20-0:35 | Scroll the consent card, click **Continue to Build GhostScore** | "She chooses what to share. We only read amounts and dates, to measure payment consistency." |
| 0:35-1:00 | Upload screen. Click **Use sample** on all three cards, then **Review added documents** | "She adds an electricity bill, a rent receipt and a UPI screenshot. In this prototype the extraction step is a stub that returns sample fields. In production, Google Document AI reads the real document." |
| 1:00-1:15 | Review screen | "Before scoring she sees what was read from each document, and any flags, so nothing is hidden." Click **Calculate my score**. |
| 1:15-1:50 | Score screen. Point at the gauge, the band row, then the confidence note | "Her score is 675, in the Good band, with a confidence note. Underneath are the signals the model leans on most. These are the model's overall weights, not a personal breakdown. Per-applicant explanations are on our roadmap." |
| 1:50-2:25 | Click **See my improvement plan** | "Now the part we care about most. Each step shows a point change that comes from re-scoring her profile with that change applied. Gemini only phrases the result. It never calculates a number. The estimates are not added together." |
| 2:25-2:40 | Back, click **Loan options for me** | "Options are drawn from cited policy passages, and clearly say that only a lender can decide." |
| 2:40-2:50 | Click **Ask the advisor**, type "How can I improve my score?" | "She can also ask questions in chat." |
| 2:50-3:00 | Switch to deck slide 6, then slide 9 | "It is designed on eight Google Cloud services. Today the prototype runs with labelled stubs, and the model is a baseline trained on public Taiwan data, so we claim no India accuracy. Next: deploy live and validate with a lender. GhostScore: making the invisible visible." |

## Things not to say

- Do not call 675 an accurate score for Priya. It comes from a heuristic feature mapping and a model trained on Taiwan 2009 data.
- Do not say Google Cloud services were called. In local mode they are all stubs.
- Do not say chat replies are generated by Gemini. They are stub text in local mode.
- Do not quote the 0.77 AUC without saying it is on the Taiwan dataset.
- Do not claim fairness testing. No fairness metrics are computed.
- Do not say Gemini is translating on screen. In local mode the Hindi and Marathi text comes from a fixed translation table (`backend/app/i18n.py`) plus UI strings in `frontend/api-client.js`. Real translation is Gemini P7 in Google Cloud mode, which has not been run.
- Do not claim the Hindi and Marathi wording is verified. It was drafted by an AI assistant and has not been reviewed by a native speaker. Have a Hindi speaker and a Marathi speaker read it before you record.
- Only the generated text and screens are translated. Names and values read from a document (for example the biller name, month names) and the text you type in chat stay as they are.
- Switch language, then wait for the page to reload: the score, plan and loan options are fetched again in the new language. The score number does not change.

## If something breaks live

- Blank score page: go back to Upload and add the sample documents again (scores are kept in the browser session).
- Server not responding: `curl localhost:8000/health` should return `{"status":"ok","mode":"local"}`. Restart with the command above.
- Chat reply looks generic: expected, it is a stub. Move on.

## If you deploy to Google Cloud first

Re-record with `GHOSTSCORE_MODE=gcp`, and replace the stub caveats above with what actually ran. Update slides 6 and 9 of the deck to match.
